## Standing is derived, not certified

A record's standing (checked, verified, replicated, contested, at risk,
stale) is worked out from what was written into it, by rules you can read in the
[spec](https://hypgraph.com/spec/) and the code. It is not a certification, a peer review, an audit, or an
endorsement by VoodooMods LLC. The rules catch some ways a record can overclaim; they cannot catch a measurement that
was wrong when it was taken, a source that misreports, or a review done carelessly.

## A verified chain proves integrity, not truth

`hypgraph verify` shows that a record or bundle has not been
changed since it was written. It does not show that what was written is correct.
