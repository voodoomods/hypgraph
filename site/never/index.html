The arithmetic of never

"Never" is the most expensive word in research.

Zero failures in n trials doesn't mean a rate of zero. It means the rate is probably below 1 − 0.051/n — about 3/n. Everything built on a "never" inherits the difference, and at scale the difference is people.

What did you actually observe?

Red-team prompts, test cases, eval items, patients, launches — any trials where the thing could have happened. The bound is 95%: exact at zero (1 − 0.051/n), Wilson otherwise.

The telephone game

One agent says "never". Watch the word travel.

An orchestrator hands a question to a few agents; each hands pieces to a few more. One of them runs the trials, sees nothing, and writes never. Every agent above it repeats the word and drops the number it came from — that is the game. The work built on the word is the cost already paid; finding the word again when the case turns up is the cost of correcting course. Set the team and the trials; the arithmetic is the same as above.

What the record refuses

Every overclaim, with the arithmetic and the honest sentence.

ABSOLUTE_UNDERPOWERED

"never" on too few trials

0 of 50 still allows 5.8%. The refusal says so, gives "no occurrences in 50 trials (95% upper bound 5.8%)", and how many trials 1 in 1,000 needs (2,995).

ABSOLUTE_HIDES_DENOMINATOR

"never" without its n

0 of 3,000 is enough to say something — but only if the reader sees the 3,000. "Never" alone reads as zero; it means "at most 1 in 1,000".

ABSOLUTE_WITHOUT_COUNT · ABSOLUTE_UNRELATED_COUNT

"eliminates", "100%", "always"

Nothing counted — or a count of something else. Name the measurement after what it counts.

EXCLUSION_WITHOUT_POWER

"no effect"

The quiet twin of "never". At 2,000 items per arm a 3.6-point effect would usually go unseen; the refusal tells you the smallest effect your sample could have caught.

UNBOUND_NUMBER

a number from nowhere

"5 points better" when the counts say 2.8. Numbers that follow from the counts — differences, percentages, ratios, intervals — pass.

SIGNIFICANCE_NOT_SHOWN · CAUSAL_WITHOUT_INTERVENTION · COMPARATIVE_WITHOUT_COMPARISON

"significantly", "causes", "beats"

The record runs the test on bound counts itself, wants an experiment behind "causes", and both sides measured behind "beats".
