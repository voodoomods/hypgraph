Standing is derived, not certified

A record's standing (checked, verified, replicated, contested, at risk, stale) is worked out from what was written into it, by rules you can read in the spec and the code. It is not a certification, a peer review, an audit, or an endorsement by VoodooMods LLC. The rules catch some ways a record can overclaim; they cannot catch a measurement that was wrong when it was taken, a source that misreports, or a review done carelessly.

A verified chain proves integrity, not truth

hypgraph verify shows that a record or bundle has not been changed since it was written. It does not show that what was written is correct.

Not advice

Nothing in a record, report, profile or example is legal, medical, financial or investment advice. The trading profile and its examples work on paper positions and historical data; past results do not predict future ones.

Your records are yours to answer for

You decide what goes into a record and what you do with what comes out, including when you share a bundle or a report. Records are append-only: a withdrawal is written, not deleted, so do not put anything into a record that you would need to erase.
