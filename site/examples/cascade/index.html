A withdrawn source · an illustration: the engine, the vendor and the team are made up. A search team decides to move to a new index engine and retire 4 of its 12 servers. Two of the three claims behind the decision are computed from the vendor’s benchmark; the third is from the team’s own load test. Then the vendor withdraws the benchmark: the cache was warm. Nobody re-reads the memo. This is the record at that moment: it marks at risk every result, claim and decision computed from the benchmark, derived on read from the links that say what each one rests on, and leaves the load-test claim standing. What happened next opens the whole record: the team re-measures on its own corpus, revises both claims, the old decision is flagged REVISIT because the claims it was based on changed, and the new decision retires 3 servers, not 4.
